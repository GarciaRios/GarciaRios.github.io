About

My research examines how Latinos develop and use portfolios of identities—and how those identities shape political behavior, policy attitudes, and participation.

Born and raised in Durango, Mexico, and a fronterizo by choice, I bring an identity-centered approach to the study of American politics. My work combines surveys, experiments, and quantitative analysis with a sustained interest in the political lives of immigrants.

That scholarship informs my teaching and my public-facing work. At Univision News, I design and interpret polling that helps explain Latino public opinion during major election cycles.
